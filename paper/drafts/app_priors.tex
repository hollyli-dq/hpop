Here $d$ is the number of realizer coordinates of $U$, and the rows of $U$ ---
one per CPA role --- are i.i.d.\ $\mathcal N(\vect 0,\Sigma_\rho)$, so that
$\rho$ correlates a role's coordinates across dimensions.  For a skill with $m$
roles,
\begin{equation}
  \log p(U\mid\rho)
  =
  -\tfrac12\Bigl[
    md\log 2\pi + m\log|\Sigma_\rho|
    + \operatorname{tr}\bigl(U\Sigma_\rho^{-1}U^\top\bigr)
  \Bigr].
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Priors.}
All priors were registered before any posterior was inspected:
\begin{align}
  \rho &\sim \mathrm{Beta}(1,1)\ \text{truncated to } (0,1-\delta_\rho),
        \quad \delta_\rho=5\times10^{-3},\\
  \beta,\ \lambda_{\mathrm{rep}},\ \lambda_{\mathrm{back}}
       &\stackrel{\text{ind}}{\sim} \mathrm{Gamma}(2,2)
        \quad\text{(shape--rate)},\\
  \omega &\sim \mathcal N(0,2^2),\\
  \vect\pi &\sim \mathrm{Dir}(\eta_\pi\vect 1_K),\qquad
  P_{h,\cdot}\sim \mathrm{Dir}(\eta_P\vect 1_{K-1}),\quad P_{hh}=0,
\end{align}
with $\eta_\pi=\eta_P=1$, so both label priors are flat; the rows of $P$ are
supported on the $(K{-}1)$-simplex of admissible successors, the diagonal being
excluded by the convention $z_{n\ell}\neq z_{n,\ell+1}$.  The truncation of
$\rho$ keeps $\Sigma_\rho$ safely positive definite and is renormalised by
$\log F_{\mathrm{Beta}}(1-\delta_\rho)$.

\paragraph{Proposal transformations.}
Each unconstrained coordinate carries its own change of variables, and the
corresponding log-Jacobian is retained in the acceptance ratio:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
coordinate & transform & proposal & log-Jacobian \\
\midrule
$U_{r,\cdot}$ (one row)
  & identity & $U_{r,\cdot}+\sigma_U\vect\xi$, $\vect\xi\sim\mathcal N(\vect0,I_d)$ & $0$ \\
$\rho$
  & $z=\operatorname{logit}\rho$ & $\rho'=\sigma(z+s_\rho\xi)$
  & $\log\rho'(1-\rho')-\log\rho(1-\rho)$ \\
$\beta,\lambda_{\mathrm{rep}},\lambda_{\mathrm{back}}$
  & $\log$ & $\theta'=\theta e^{s\xi}$ & $\log\theta'-\log\theta$ \\
$\omega$
  & identity & $\omega+s_\omega\xi$ & $0$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
with $\xi\sim\mathcal N(0,1)$ and $\sigma_U=0.5$.  Structural moves select one
skill and one role row uniformly.  Dropping the $\log$-scale correction visibly
shifts the stationary distribution; a negative control for each Jacobian is
retained in the test suite.

\paragraph{Conjugate updates.}
$\vect\pi$ and $P$ are not proposed but drawn directly from their conditional
posteriors, which are Dirichlet by conjugacy:
\begin{equation}
  \vect\pi\mid Z \sim \mathrm{Dir}\bigl(\eta_\pi+n_k\bigr)_{k=1}^{K},
  \qquad
  P_{h,\cdot}\mid Z \sim \mathrm{Dir}\bigl(\eta_P+C_{hk}\bigr)_{k\neq h},
\end{equation}
where $n_k$ counts first segments of type $k$ and $C_{hk}$ counts $h\to k$
transitions across all traces.

\paragraph{Normalising constants.}
As with $|\Sigma_\rho|$ above, the Gamma, Gaussian and Dirichlet normalisers are
omitted identically on every call and cancel in every ratio the sampler forms;
they must be restored if the corresponding hyperparameters are themselves
updated.

\paragraph{Coordinates held fixed.}
In the reported FULL-LATENT runs only $(S_n,z_n,U,\vect\pi,P)$ are inferred; the
recurrent nuisance coordinates are fixed at $\rho=0.5$, $\beta=1.5$,
$\omega=\log(0.85/0.15)$, $\lambda_{\mathrm{rep}}=0.8$,
$\lambda_{\mathrm{back}}=0.25$, with $\epsilon=0.02$ and $\delta_B=0.15$, so the
scalar priors above are registered but not exercised there.
