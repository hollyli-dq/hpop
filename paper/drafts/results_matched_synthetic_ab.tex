\subsection{Component identifiability under the matched model}
\label{sec:matched-synthetic-ab}

We first ask whether each latent component of HPOP is recoverable at all, under
a generator that implements the inference target exactly. The generator was
validated before any recovery experiment: its segmentation prior is the
normalised
$p(S \mid J, \delta_B) = \delta_B^{L-1}(1-\delta_B)^{J-L} / C_J(\delta_B)$
over contiguous segmentations with widths in $[3,12]$, sampled by an exact
suffix-DP recursion, and all fifteen registered gates pass
(Appendix~\ref{app:generator}). All experiments in this subsection use one
frozen corpus: $100$ training traces ($25$ each at $J \in \{24,32,40,48\}$) and
$45$ held-out traces, $785$ true skill-instance blocks, repeat-occurrence
frequency $0.512$.

The two conditions below are deliberately \emph{conditional}. Condition~A fixes
the reusable structures $H$ and every global parameter to generating truth and
asks whether the skill-instance path $(S, z)$ is recoverable. Condition~B fixes
$(S,z)$ to generating truth and asks whether $H$ is recoverable. Neither is a
joint-recovery result, and we are explicit about that in
\S\ref{par:remains-for-joint}.

\paragraph{Path identifiability with oracle structures.}
Condition~A requires no MCMC. With $H$, $\pi$, $P$ and all scalars fixed to
truth, the posterior $p(S, z \mid x, \cdot)$ factorises over traces and is
computed \emph{exactly} by semi-Markov forward--backward; FFBS is used only to
draw i.i.d.\ samples for non-linear summaries. Exactness is certified on a tiny
enumerable corpus: the DP normaliser matches brute-force enumeration to
$1.8\times10^{-15}$, exact path probabilities to $2.3\times10^{-15}$, boundary
marginals to $1.7\times10^{-15}$, and $200{,}000$ FFBS draws per trace match the
enumerated path distribution to a total variation of $0.002$. Across the full
corpus, $725{,}000$ FFBS draws produced zero illegal segmentations.

The posterior is sharply informative about boundaries. Boundary AUROC is
$0.956$ overall and $0.966$ on held-out traces, against a prior AUROC of
$0.579$; boundary Brier falls from $0.108$ to $0.052$ (a $52.4\%$ relative
reduction) and boundary NLL from $0.363$ to $0.166$ ($54.2\%$). Calibration is
good in absolute terms, with an expected calibration error of $0.008$ over ten
bins at a fixed, never-tuned threshold; at that threshold precision is $0.782$,
recall $0.609$ and $F_1$ $0.685$ ($0.713$ held out). Occurrence labels are
recovered at $0.898$ modal accuracy with mean posterior mass $0.854$ on the true
label, against prior values of $0.384$ and $0.339$; the mean per-trace
adjusted Rand index is $0.747$ and NMI $0.766$, against a prior ARI of $0.131$.
The posterior segment count is right at MAP on $70.3\%$ of traces ($77.8\%$
held out) with a mean $|\mathbb{E}[L] - L^\ast|$ of $0.439$.

Whole-path recovery is the honest weak point and we report it as such. The MAP
labelled path equals truth exactly on $20.7\%$ of traces and the median
posterior mass on the true path is $0.048$. This is not a contradiction: the
exact posterior removes a mean of $10.54$ nats of path entropy (from $14.02$ to
$3.48$), but on long traces that mass is spread over near-equivalent paths that
differ in one boundary. The effect is monotone in trace length --- exact MAP
recovery is $0.324$ at $J=24$, $0.250$ at $J=32$ and $0.083$ at $J=48$ --- while
marginal accuracy is essentially flat (boundary AUROC $0.973$, $0.943$, $0.955$
and $0.955$ across the four lengths). The preregistered strong-verdict rule
required either MAP whole-path recovery $\geq 0.25$ \emph{or} median true-path
posterior $\geq 0.02$; the second disjunct is met and the first is not. Every
other strong gate is met with margin. The registered classification is therefore
\textsc{path strongly identifiable}, and it should be read as a statement about
marginals and calibration rather than about exact whole-path decoding.

\paragraph{Structure identifiability with oracle paths.}
Condition~B inverts the conditioning. With $(S,z)$ fixed to the oracle blocks
($541$ training blocks), the target is
$p(U \mid X, S^\ast, z^\ast, \vartheta^\ast, \rho_0)
\propto \prod_k p(U_k \mid \rho_0)
\prod_{n,l} e_{n, z^\ast_{nl}}(a^\ast_{nl}, b^\ast_{nl}; h(U), \vartheta^\ast)$,
sampled by a symmetric Gaussian row walk with Metropolis--Hastings on the
oracle-block likelihood. The step scale $\sigma_U = 0.5$ was chosen by an
efficiency-only pilot (acceptance $0.314$, maximum ESJD in the registered
$[0.2, 0.6]$ band); all pilot draws were discarded and recovery was never
inspected during tuning.

Three facts must be stated before the numbers. First, $\rho$ was \emph{fixed} at
$\rho_0 = 0.5$ by registered correction, because the supplied-truth corpus
records $\rho^\ast = \texttt{null}$; $\rho$ was not a recovery target and we make
no $\rho$ claim. The value $0.5$ sits near the median of the registered
$\mathrm{Uniform}(0, 0.995)$ prior and gives a positive-definite $\Sigma$ with
moderate column coupling; it was frozen before any draw. Second, raw entrywise
recovery of $U$ was \emph{not} evaluated and is not a meaningful target: the
latent product-order coordinates are exchangeable and the likelihood reads $U$
only through $H = h(U)$. Third, the held-out likelihood reported here is an
\emph{oracle-conditional} quantity, scored with the held-out $S^\ast, z^\ast$.

Four chains of $50{,}000$ sweeps were run from four dispersed starts
(scales $0.5$, $1.0$, $2.0$, $3.0$), all mutually distinct and none equal to the
truth. The run stopped at $50{,}000$ on two consecutive checkpoint passes, well
inside its $100{,}000$ ceiling, with a maximum $\widehat{R}$ of $1.0009$, a
log-posterior bulk ESS of $3675$ and tail ESS of $7635$, in $203$~s of wall time.
Recovery is exact. For all three skills the posterior mass on the true $H$ is
$1.000$ and the true $H$ is rank $1$; closure $F_1$, incomparable-pair $F_1$ and
transitive-reduction $F_1$ are all $1.000$; the closure Hamming distance to
truth is $0$; the minimum true-relation marginal is $1.000$ and the maximum
false-relation marginal $0.000$. Held-out oracle-path NLL per occurrence is
$1.0772$ under the posterior mean, indistinguishable from the generating truth
at $1.0772$, against $1.7528$ for an antichain and $4.0398$ for a total order.

The retained draws contain a constant $H$, and it is important to say why. This
is posterior \emph{concentration}, not a stuck sampler. All four chains began at
distinct, wrong $H$ tuples; every chain accepted its first structural change at
sweep~1; and the chains accepted $25$, $30$, $31$ and $24$ structural changes
respectively --- all of them inside the $10{,}000$-sweep burn-in, as each chain
walked from its own wrong start into the same true $H$ cell. Zero retained
structural changes is what a correctly mixing sampler looks like once every
chain has found a posterior that puts unit mass on one cell. The classification
is \textsc{structure strongly identifiable under oracle paths}.

\paragraph{What remains for joint inference.}
\label{par:remains-for-joint}
Conditions~A and~B together establish that each component is identifiable
\emph{given the other}. Condition~A identifies $(S, z)$ while $H$ and the global
parameters are held at truth; Condition~B identifies $H$ while $(S, z)$ are held
at truth. Neither result, and no combination of them, establishes that the two
are jointly recoverable when both are latent, because the obstruction we have
previously measured is a coupling between them rather than a deficiency in
either. Earlier work on this model recorded exactly that obstruction: exact
global FFBS refreshes of $(S,z)$ alone moved segmentation vigorously (over $500$
boundary flips per sweep) while producing $0$, $0$, $0$ and $1$ accepted
structural changes across four chains and $34{,}000$ retained sweeps each. The
path-marginal (collapsed-$U$) kernel of \S\ref{sec:inference} was built for that
coupling and reduces the median cross-structure log-likelihood barrier from
$-225.8$ to $-69.3$ nats, raising cross-structure acceptance from
$\mathcal{O}(10^{-14})$ to $\approx 7\times 10^{-2}$; in its validated form it
visits over $3{,}500$ distinct induced structures per chain where the
FFBS-only kernel visits one. Whether that translates into correct joint
posterior recovery of $(S, z, H)$ is a separate empirical question, and it is
the question Condition~C asks.
