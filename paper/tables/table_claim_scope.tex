\begin{table*}[p]
\centering
\footnotesize
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
\caption{%
Scope of every experimental component: what is tested, what is thereby
established, and --- as importantly --- what is not. Three evidence classes are
distinguished throughout: \textsc{full hpop} (the complete generative model
under inference), \textsc{component} (one part of the model, in isolation), and
\textsc{pending} (no frozen artifact exists; no outcome is implied).%
}
\label{tab:claim-scope}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l l X X l@{}}
\toprule
Component & Class & What is tested / established & What is \emph{not} established & Location \\
\midrule

Exact FFBS
& \textsc{component} \newline (inference kernel)
& That the labelled-path conditional posterior $p(S, z \mid x, \cdot)$ is
  sampled exactly. Tiny-space DP-versus-enumeration agreement to
  $\leq 2.6\times10^{-15}$ on log-normalisers, path probabilities, boundary
  marginals and occurrence-label marginals; FFBS path TV $0.002$ at
  $200{,}000$ draws/trace; $0$ illegal draws in $725{,}000$ corpus draws.
& Nothing about $H$, about global parameters, or about joint mixing. Exactness
  is certified numerically on an enumerable corpus, not proved.
& \S\ref{sec:inference}, App.~A \\

Path-marginal ($U$-collapsed) kernel
& \textsc{component} \newline (inference kernel)
& That a collapsed MH move on $U$ followed by an \emph{immediate} exact FFBS
  refresh is stationary for the joint target (finite-state deviation
  $2.2\times10^{-16}$), and that interposing any stale-path update breaks
  stationarity ($2.9\times10^{-3}$ negative control). Full-scale validation
  passes all $17$ frozen distributional and convergence gates plus a
  dependence-aware energy gate at $500{,}000$ sweeps.
& That the kernel recovers the truth. Validation is a \emph{correctness}
  result against references, not a recovery result. Two historical runs FAILED
  the earlier IID-calibrated energy gate ($z = +2.86$, $+5.76$) and those
  verdicts stand; the gate was replaced only after a separate calibration study
  showed its null was miscalibrated for autocorrelated draws.
& \S\ref{sec:inference}, App.~B \\

Matched generator
& \textsc{component} \newline (matched synthetic)
& That the generator implements the inference target exactly: the normalised
  segmentation prior via exact suffix-DP, per-block recurrent reset, zero
  self-transitions. $15/15$ registered gates pass; $4/4$ negative controls
  detected; generator/scorer complete-data parity $1.4\times10^{-14}$.
& Anything about recovery. A perfectly matched generator makes a subsequent
  failure interpretable; it is not itself evidence for the model.
& App.~C \\

Condition A
& \textsc{full hpop} \newline (conditional)
& Path identifiability under oracle structures: boundary AUROC $0.956$
  ($0.966$ held out), Brier $0.052$ ($-52.4\%$ vs prior), ECE $0.008$,
  occurrence accuracy $0.898$, ARI $0.747$, segment-count MAP accuracy $0.703$.
& Joint recovery. $H$, $\pi$, $P$ and every scalar are fixed at truth. Exact
  whole-path MAP recovery is only $0.207$ and falls to $0.083$ at $J = 48$;
  the strong verdict rests on marginals and calibration.
& \S\ref{sec:matched-synthetic-ab} \\

Condition B
& \textsc{full hpop} \newline (conditional)
& Structure identifiability under oracle paths: closure, incomparable and
  reduction $F_1$ all $1.000$, closure Hamming $0$, posterior mass on true $H$
  $= 1.000$ for all three skills, held-out oracle-path NLL indistinguishable
  from truth ($1.077$ vs $1.077$; antichain $1.753$, total order $4.040$),
  $\widehat{R}_{\max} = 1.001$.
& Joint recovery; recovery of $\rho$ (fixed at $\rho_0 = 0.5$ by registered
  correction, $\rho^\ast = $ null); raw entrywise $U$ recovery (not a target ---
  the likelihood reads $U$ only through $h(U)$); scaling beyond $K = 3$ skills
  and $m = 5$ roles.
& \S\ref{sec:matched-synthetic-ab} \\

Condition C
& \textsc{pending}
& \emph{Nothing yet.} Frozen design facts only: both arms provably target the
  same posterior (small-reference equality PASS), conditional $U$ scale $0.5$,
  scheduled collapsed scale $1.0$, cadence $c = 10$.
& Everything. Joint recoverability of $(S, z, H)$ is exactly the open question.
  No outcome may be inferred from the run's existence or duration.
& \textit{pending} \\

TaskBench
& \textsc{component} \newline (static partial order)
& That the static product-order component generalises to unseen legal
  linearizations on external gold graphs: monotone
  \textsc{one}$\to$\textsc{two}$\to$\textsc{four} improvement in closure $F_1$
  ($0.452 \to 0.902$), incomparable $F_1$, Brier, held-out legal NLL and AUROC,
  with all \textsc{four}$-$\textsc{one} intervals excluding zero; $132/132$
  fits converged.
& Full HPOP; segmentation; reusable skill instances; recurrent repair;
  hierarchy; library learning. Also \emph{not} a point-recovery win:
  order-intersection is ahead on closure $F_1$ at \textsc{one} and \textsc{two}
  and tied at \textsc{four}, and every AUROC difference covers zero. The
  linearizations are enumerated from gold DAGs, not observed trajectories.
& \S\ref{sec:taskbench}, App.~D \\

$\tau^3$-Retail
& \textsc{pending} \newline (setup frozen)
& \emph{Setup only.} A frozen, task-disjoint, externally sourced corpus:
  $456$ extracted trajectories, $30$ tasks / $119$ trajectories selected,
  $79$/$20$/$20$ train/dev/sealed-test, $8$-tool exact-name CPA vocabulary,
  median length $7$, repeated-CPA fraction $0.924$, $103$ successes /
  $16$ failures; $24/24$ construction gates pass; version alignment verified
  against the pinned official benchmark.
& Any predictive claim whatsoever. No HPOP fit result exists. The corpus has no
  gold segmentation and no gold partial order. Trajectories are a third-party
  public release from one specific agent configuration, not an official
  maintainer release.
& \S\ref{sec:tau3-setup} \\

\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table*}
