\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}
\footnotesize
\textbf{Classifications.}
A: \textsc{path strongly identifiable} (all preregistered strong gates met;
the path-evidence gate was met by median true-path posterior $0.048 \geq 0.02$,
not by MAP whole-path recovery, which is $0.207 < 0.25$).
B: \textsc{structure strongly identifiable under oracle paths}.
C: \textit{pending} --- no frozen artifact exists and no outcome is implied.

\textbf{Which quantities are oracle-conditional.}
Condition~A conditions on oracle $H, \pi, P, \rho, \beta, \omega,
\lambda_{\mathrm{rep}}, \lambda_{\mathrm{back}}, \delta_B, \epsilon$; its
boundary and label metrics are exact posterior summaries under that
conditioning.
Condition~B conditions on oracle $(S^\ast, z^\ast)$ and fixes $\rho$ at
$\rho_0 = 0.5$ by registered correction; $\rho$ was not a recovery target and
raw entrywise $U$ recovery was not evaluated, because the likelihood reads $U$
only through $H = h(U)$.

$^{\dagger}$\,\textbf{Held-out NLL is not one quantity across rows.}
Condition~B's $1.077$ nats/occurrence is an \emph{oracle-path} predictive score,
computed with the held-out $S^\ast, z^\ast$ supplied (generating truth
$1.077$; antichain baseline $1.753$; total-order baseline $4.040$). Condition~C
will report a \emph{marginal} held-out NLL with $(S,z)$ latent. The two are
different quantities and must never be averaged, differenced or plotted on a
shared axis.

\textbf{Condition~A boundary $F_1$} is measured at a fixed, never-tuned
threshold of $0.5$ (precision $0.782$, recall $0.609$); boundary AUROC is
$0.956$ overall and $0.966$ held out.
\textbf{Condition~B recovery} is exact on all three skills (closure Hamming
distance $0$, posterior mass on the true structure $1.000$, transitive-reduction
$F_1$ $1.000$); the constant retained structure reflects posterior
concentration, since all four chains started from distinct wrong structures and
accepted $24$--$31$ structural changes each during burn-in.
