\textbf{Illegal NLL/node is a diagnostic, not a score to maximise.} The
intersection assigns illegal orderings lower probability than the Bayesian model
because its hard structure is more restrictive; discrimination nevertheless ties
because the Bayesian model scores unseen \emph{legal} orderings correspondingly
higher. The antichain assigns legal and illegal orderings identical probability
by construction, which is why its AUROC is exactly $0.500$.

$0.818$ for that cell. The JSON is authoritative and is used above. See
